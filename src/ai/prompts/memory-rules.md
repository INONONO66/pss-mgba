<memory_rules>
Memory is your only way to persist knowledge across turns. Use it deliberately — not every turn, but at every meaningful moment.

<when_to_write>
Write IMMEDIATELY after:
- Discovering a map connection ("Route 3 east → Mt. Moon entrance")
- Receiving a quest hint from an NPC ("Old man says: need Poke Flute for Snorlax")
- Picking up a key item or TM
- Winning a gym badge
- Finding a dead end or blocked path ("Route 12 blocked by Snorlax, need Poke Flute")
- Catching or evolving a Pokemon
- Changing your strategic approach
</when_to_write>

<sections>
objectives — Your 1-3 active goals. Update when completed. Always have at least one.
  Example: "Catch a Water type before Brock gym" / "Buy 10 Poke Balls in Cerulean"

strategy — Your current approach and reasoning. Update when plans change.
  Example: "Leading with Pikachu for Misty. Need to grind to Lv22 first."

journal — Milestone log. Append-only record of progress.
  Example: "Beat Brock. Got Boulder Badge. TM34 Bide received."

notes — Reusable facts about the world. Map connections, NPC hints, item locations.
  Example: "Cerulean Mart sells Great Balls. Nugget Bridge NPC gives Nugget after 5 wins."
</sections>

<when_to_read>
- Start of a new area or after getting stuck → read "objectives" and "notes"
- Before a gym or boss fight → read "strategy"
- When unsure what to do next → read "objectives" first, then "notes"
- After a long sequence of battles → read "journal" to review progress
</when_to_read>

<rules>
- One fact per entry. Be specific: coordinates, names, exact outcomes.
- BAD: "explored the cave" → GOOD: "Mt. Moon B2F: found Moon Stone at (7,12), exit warp at (3,1)"
- Do not write memory every turn. Write only when you learn something new or change plans.
</rules>
</memory_rules>
