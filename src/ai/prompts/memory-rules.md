Memory Rules
- Use pokemon_memory_write after discovering: a new map exit, an NPC with unique dialog, an item pickup, a story event trigger, a dead-end path.
- Write to "strategy" when your approach changes: new route plan, different target, grinding decision.
- Write to "journal" after milestones: badge obtained, key item found, new area reached, battle won.
- Write to "notes" for reusable observations: which exits connect where, which NPCs are story-relevant, which items are in which locations.
- Use pokemon_memory_read to review findings before choosing a new direction after getting stuck.
- Limit each memory entry to one fact or decision. Prefer concrete coordinates and names over vague descriptions.

=== WHAT TO RECORD ===

Team and Catches (write to "journal"):
- Record every Pokemon you catch: species, level, location, and why it was caught (type gap, HM, gym counter).
- Record evolutions and key level-ups (e.g., "Charmeleon evolved into Charizard at Lv36").
- Record your current team composition after any roster change.

Shopping and Economy (write to "notes"):
- Record which Poke Mart you visited and what items were available.
- Record large purchases or sales so you know your remaining budget.
- Record where you found free items (overworld pickups, gifted items from NPCs).

Gym and Battle Intel (write to "strategy"):
- Before a gym, record your plan: which Pokemon and moves you will lead with.
- After a gym, record what worked and what did not for future reference.
- Record trainer types and levels encountered in new areas to gauge difficulty.

Exploration (write to "notes"):
- Record map connections: "Route 3 east exit leads to Mt. Moon entrance".
- Record blocked paths and what might unblock them: "Snorlax blocks Route 12, need Poke Flute".
- Record NPC hints about where to go next or what items are needed.

Objectives (write to "objectives"):
- Keep 1-3 active objectives at all times. Update when completed.
- Example: "Catch a Grass type for Misty gym", "Buy 10 Poke Balls in Cerulean", "Grind team to Lv18 before gym".
- Clear completed objectives and replace with the next goal.

=== WHEN TO READ MEMORY ===

- Read "objectives" at the start of each decision cycle to stay focused.
- Read "strategy" when entering a gym or difficult area.
- Read "notes" when stuck or deciding which direction to explore.
- Read "journal" to check party composition and recent progress before major decisions.
