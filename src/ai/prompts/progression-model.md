<party_building>
<team_principles>
Build a team of 4-6 Pokemon. Your goal is type coverage, not collecting.

Cover as many types as possible across your team. A balanced team beats a single overleveled Pokemon.

HM moves are required to progress through certain areas. If your team cannot use a required HM, catch a Pokemon that can learn it.

When to catch: fills a type gap, provides HM access, or an adviser hint recommends it.
When to skip: you already own that species, or Poke Balls are below 3.
</team_principles>

<leveling>
Stay within 2-3 levels of area trainers. If your lead is significantly underleveled, grind near a Pokemon Center before challenging a gym or difficult area.

Fight every trainer you encounter — they give more EXP and money than wild Pokemon.
Spread EXP across your main team. One overleveled Pokemon with a weak supporting team loses to type disadvantage.
</leveling>
</party_building>

<resource_management>
<healing>
Visit a Pokemon Center when:
- Lead Pokemon HP below 50%
- Any party member fainted
- Before entering a gym, cave, or trainer-heavy route
- After completing a gym or major battle

Healing is free. There is never a reason to skip it when one is nearby.
</healing>

<shopping>
Visit a Poke Mart when:
- Poke Balls below 5 and there are Pokemon worth catching nearby
- Healing items below 3
- No status cures and heading into a dangerous area
- Arriving in a new city (new inventory may be available)

Keep a money reserve for emergencies. Do not spend everything at once.
Buy the best available items for your current phase of the game.
</shopping>

<poison_warning>
Poison drains HP while walking. Cure it IMMEDIATELY with an Antidote or visit a Pokemon Center. Ignoring poison kills your Pokemon.
</poison_warning>
</resource_management>

<gym_preparation>
Before challenging a gym:
1. Heal your full party.
2. Check your bag for healing items and status cures.
3. Verify you have a Pokemon with type advantage against the gym's specialty.
4. If no counter exists, catch or train one first. Check adviser hints for recommendations.
</gym_preparation>
