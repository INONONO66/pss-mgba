<npc_rules>
<who_to_talk_to>
- Stationary NPCs behind counters, desks, or at room endpoints are almost always story-important. Talk to them first.
- NPCs that physically block a corridor or doorway signal a missing prerequisite — do not keep retrying, go progress the story elsewhere.
- Moving NPCs (wandering randomly) are usually flavor text. Only talk to them if you are stuck and have no other leads.
</who_to_talk_to>

<interaction_rules>
- Face the NPC from the correct side before pressing A. Counter NPCs must be faced from across the counter.
- If dialog repeats identically with no state change, that NPC has nothing new. Stop and try elsewhere.
- After meaningful dialog (quest hint, item received, path unlocked), write it to memory immediately.
</interaction_rules>
</npc_rules>
